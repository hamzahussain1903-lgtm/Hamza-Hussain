Selected Case Studies

01 — Operational Performance Improvement

Context: As a Deputy Team Leader at DWP, I identified team productivity at 47%, with high video non-attendance and repeat follow-ups affecting delivery.

  • Analysed Power BI and management information to identify where time and productivity were being lost.
  • Identified repeat follow-ups and missed video appointments as key operational issues.
  • Introduced pre-calls and targeted activity to improve appointment preparation and customer engagement.
  • Used a Gantt chart to prioritise actions and monitored performance to test whether changes worked.

Outcome: Productivity increased from 47% to 84%; video DNA reduced from 26% to 11%; repeat follow-ups fell by 22%; customer satisfaction increased by 15%.

02 — Process Improvement & Quality Assurance

Context: I identified delays and inconsistencies in childcare support processing, creating additional work for colleagues and uncertainty for customers.

  • Reviewed the existing process and identified inconsistent evidence requests and recording.
  • Created clearer process guidance and coached colleagues through the changes.
  • Monitored quality and encouraged complex cases to be raised early.

Outcome: Processing times reduced by 65%, while 95% of colleagues reported confidence in following the revised process.

03 — Evidence-Based Risk & Decision Support

Context: During a housing-cost evidence appointment, I identified concerns with tenancy agreements submitted by a claimant.

  • Reviewed documents and identified inconsistencies in formatting, figures and information.
  • Cross-referenced tenancy details against the Universal Credit record and identified further discrepancies.
  • Requested supporting evidence and referred the case to the Decision Maker with a clear evidence-based rationale.

Outcome: Unsupported housing costs were not accepted and a £1,600 overpayment was identified.
